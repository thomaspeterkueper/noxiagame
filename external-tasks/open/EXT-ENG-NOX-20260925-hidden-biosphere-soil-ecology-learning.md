# EXTERNAL TASK — Hidden Biosphere / soil ecology gameplay + learning

- **Origin:** Engineering / OTA / NOXIA Universe
- **Target:** NOXIA Game
- **Date:** 2026-09-25
- **Status:** open

## Request
Design fungi and soil ecology as functional systems rather than decorative flora.

Agriculture and habitat ecology should depend on substrate state, water, nutrients, organic matter, microbial/fungal communities and plant associations. Allow mycorrhizal/decomposer networks to affect nutrient availability, resilience and recovery while retaining uncertainty and species/context dependence.

Integrate with Mars settlement, closed-loop life support, waste streams, energy, Planetary Evolution and future ecopoiesis.

## Learning module — Hidden Biosphere
Begin with visible fruiting bodies and lead the player to discover the larger hidden mycelial/substrate system. Let players inspect soil/substrate conditions, nutrient cycling, decomposition and plant associations.

Include a second investigation in which an unusual species observation (inspired by the Sauerland Kaiserling case) generates competing habitat/climate/dispersal hypotheses. Require repeated observations and environmental context rather than equating one sighting with climate proof.

For Mars, teach why 'grow plants in regolith' is not equivalent to establishing a functioning soil ecosystem.
