# NOXIA Request: Biological Robustness & Space Biology

Origin: Biological Robustness Engineering, 2026-10-08
Priority: medium-high

Integrate a future research/simulation model for biological robustness in long-duration space environments.

Do not implement "radiation resistance" as one scalar. Model layered mechanisms:
exposure/shielding -> molecular damage prevention -> damage burden -> sensing/response -> repair -> mitochondrial/proteostatic maintenance -> senescence/clearance -> tissue recovery -> functional outcome.

Candidate stressors: radiation quality/dose/time structure, microgravity, isolation/closed habitat effects where biologically relevant, hypoxia, temperature/resource stress and combined exposures.

Tardigrade/Dsup biology is a research inspiration/evidence case, not a justification for fictional instant human enhancement.

Connect with NPC/body physiology only when the underlying body model can support the relevant timescales and state variables. Preserve computational efficiency: slow biological processes should use scheduled/coarse-grained updates rather than per-frame simulation.

Potential research use must remain clearly separated from gameplay and require appropriate consent/governance for human-derived data.
