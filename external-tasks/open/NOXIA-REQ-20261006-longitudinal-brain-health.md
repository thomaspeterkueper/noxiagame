# NOXIA Request: Longitudinal Brain Health systems model

Origin: research synthesis, 2026-10-06
Dependency: KG/SSF mappings
Priority: high

Design a scientifically grounded brain-health knowledge/research layer based on interacting life-course systems rather than a single dementia-risk score.

Candidate domains: sensory function, cardiometabolic state, physical activity, cognitive engagement, social connectedness, sleep/context, environmental exposure and emerging longitudinal markers such as speech/language changes. Cellular maintenance can provide a deeper biological layer.

Requirements:
- no deterministic “dementia score”
- population evidence must not be presented as individual diagnosis
- explicit research consent for player-derived longitudinal measurements
- retain temporal context and within-person baselines
- account for geography/culture/equity and accessibility
- use canonical KG entities and SSF explanations
- distinguish established prevention evidence from exploratory research mechanics.
