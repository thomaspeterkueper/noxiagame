**Origin:** cross-project research intake

Integrate gold as a scientifically grounded multi-scale material/knowledge thread without inventing new canonical science. Candidate gameplay/worldbuilding links:
- resource extraction, refining, recycling and scarcity,
- electronics/contacts and high-reliability systems,
- sensors/photonics/nanotechnology where supported,
