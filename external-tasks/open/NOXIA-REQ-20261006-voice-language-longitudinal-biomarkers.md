# NOXIA Request: Voice & Language longitudinal research framework

**Origin:** research intake, 2026-10-06
**Dependency:** KG canonical mappings and research governance
**Priority:** high

## Goal
Assess whether consenting NOXIA research participants could contribute to longitudinal research on changes in speech and language without turning gameplay into covert health screening.

## Candidate feature layers
- acoustic voice features
- speech timing, pauses and prosody
- linguistic/semantic features
- longitudinal within-person change vectors

## Design requirements
- explicit, separate research consent; no inference from ordinary players without consent
- research use, not diagnosis
- prefer within-person longitudinal baselines over simplistic “sounds old” classifications
- record task/context/language and relevant technical metadata
- separate raw audio from derived features; minimise retention of identifiable raw voice
- support withdrawal/deletion and purpose limitation
- do not expose health-like scores to NPCs or gameplay systems
- evaluate multilingual/cultural bias before comparison across populations
- define preregisterable hypotheses and validation against external measures before claiming predictive value

## Scientific anchor
Hernandez et al., Science Advances 2026, DOI 10.1126/sciadv.aef9864. The current evidence is primarily cross-sectional and is not a validated dementia diagnostic/prediction system.
