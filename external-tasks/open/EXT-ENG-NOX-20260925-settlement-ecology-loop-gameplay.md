- **Origin:** Engineering / Mycology / Planetary Evolution
- **Target:** NOXIA Game
- **Date:** 2026-09-25
- **Status:** open

## Request
Unify habitat life support, agriculture, organic waste and soil ecology into visible material loops.

Players should be able to trace at least water, carbon/oxygen, nitrogen/phosphorus and organic matter through settlement systems. Fungi/microbes should perform functional decomposition/mineralization roles rather than grant generic bonuses.

Early settlements may rely heavily on physicochemical recycling and imported substrate/nutrients; later systems can become more biologically integrated but also gain ecological failure modes.

Add diagnostics that distinguish inventory shortage, flow bottleneck and biological dysfunction.

Connect with Energy Grid, adaptive industry, MiniNodes/robotics and Hidden Biosphere learning modules.
