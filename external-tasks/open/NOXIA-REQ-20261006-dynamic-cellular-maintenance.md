# NOXIA Request: Dynamic Cellular Maintenance biology model

Origin: cross-project research intake, 2026-10-06
Dependency: KG/SSF science mappings
Priority: medium

Integrate cellular maintenance into NOXIA's biology/medicine knowledge layer where appropriate: autophagy, lysosomal recycling, mitophagy, proteostasis, senescence and immune clearance.
