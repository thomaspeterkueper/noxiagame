## Request
Extend procedural planet generation from static properties toward internally coherent histories.

Generate hidden timelines linking tectonic regime/orogeny, erosion/weathering, hydrology/sedimentation, nutrient availability, ocean redox/oxygen, atmospheric evolution and biosphere state. Do not directly roll 'complex biosphere' independently of environmental history.

Preserve hidden ground truth separately from what players/scientists can reconstruct from geological and biological evidence.

## Deep Time learning module
Create an optional investigation using zircon ages/provenance, sediment cores, nutrient/redox proxies, isotope evidence and fossils. Players should:
1. establish a chronology;
2. reconstruct an ancient mountain-building/erosion episode;
3. identify changes in nutrient delivery and productivity;
4. compare oxygen/redox evidence and biological diversification;
5. test alternative causal models;
6. vary selected model parameters and observe consequences over geological timescales.

Teach correlation versus causation, proxies, uncertainty and coupled Earth/planet-system behavior rather than presenting one explanation as certain.

Cross-link with Planetary Remote Sensing and future terraforming gameplay.
