# Q1 lived society foundation

Q1 is a settlement of roughly 3,200–3,500 permanent residents, not a stage built around Tirin Kal. Tirin is one resident and must encounter a society whose people, institutions and routines exist independently of him.

## Population model
Use the existing Living Population identity/assignment/need/relationship/knowledge systems. Named people are sparse anchors; most residents remain cohorts/background until simulation LOD requires individual identity. Do not create thousands of decorative rows.

Life stages must become explicit simulation context: early childhood, learning-age child, adolescent, adult and older adult. Age affects obligations, autonomy, care, learning, work and mobility; children must not use adult employment logic unchanged.

## Learning and care
Q1 needs a **Learning Commons** rather than a 2020s classroom school copied into space. It combines:
- early-childhood care and play rooms;
- mixed-age project studios;
- simulation/VR laboratories;
- practical station-systems learning;
- quiet study and remote instruction;
- human mentors, specialist teachers and AI tutors;
- apprenticeships and supervised participation in real station work for older learners.

The institution still fulfils the social functions of kindergarten and school: dependable care, peer groups, safeguarding, foundational learning, meals, movement and a daily rhythm.

## Ordinary public life
Model reusable public institutions, not Tirin-specific scenery: clinic, food hall/market, cafés, recreation and sport, gardens, library/archive, maker/repair spaces, civic rooms, performance/media spaces, quiet rooms, observation spaces, transfer nodes and harbour.

Station life should generate ordinary events: shifts, deliveries, maintenance closures, lessons, celebrations, performances, arrivals/departures, disputes, illness, repairs, births, friendships, clubs, research projects and local news.

## Animals and social machines
Do not assume Earth-style unrestricted pet ownership. Distinguish:
1. **biological companion animals** — species and numbers constrained by welfare, habitat volume, allergens, disease control and life-support cost;
2. **ecological animals** — present because a managed habitat/ecosystem needs them, not as props;
3. **robopets/social robots** — common where residents want companionship without biological life-support burden.

Robopets are not Persons. Biological animals also need their own agent/welfare model rather than being encoded as human NPCs.

## Tirin
No privileged encounters, predetermined friends or automatic writing. His biography emerges from the same places, schedules, co-presence, knowledge and memory rules as other residents. Writing is later an explicit action that consumes time and selects only material he actually knows or remembers.
