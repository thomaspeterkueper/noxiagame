# Tharsis Hub — human capability gap plan r1

Status: NOXIA runtime planning contract

Tharsis Hub is currently assessed from its canonical seed as `adult-survival-capable`. The existing habitat clusters, ECLSS hubs, documented habitat Safe-Haven function and general medical facilities are sufficient for that NOXIA label. They do not establish pregnancy/birth, child-development or multigenerational capability.

## Modular closure

The next capability layer is intentionally attached to existing infrastructure rather than represented as three magic unlock buildings.

- **Perinatal care suite** extends `medical_core` / `medical_annex` with pregnancy monitoring, operative obstetrics and neonatal stabilization/care.
- **Child development zone** extends habitat clusters with protected everyday movement, play, sleep and age-appropriate living space.
- **Development services** connect school and medical services for education plus longitudinal developmental monitoring.
- **Gravity transition service** connects medical and habitat functions for assessment, conditioning and rehabilitation around gravity transitions.

These are functional modules, not yet building definitions. They define no costs, build ticks, staffing numbers, fertility/birth rates, mortality effects, safe gravity threshold or prescribed artificial-gravity dose.

## Boundary

Radiation protection remains a separate infrastructure axis. Artificial gravity remains an optional technical architecture signal and is not a biological unlock. A later gameplay/content pass may map these modules to rooms, interiors, equipment and staffing only when the corresponding NOXIA/Engineering authority exists.
